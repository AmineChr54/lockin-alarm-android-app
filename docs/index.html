Before bed, it checks itself.

Eight hours before it rings, a notification tells you if anything would go wrong tonight: NFC, notifications, Do Not Disturb, battery, volume, tag.

From here, it only gets stricter.

The commitment lock closes hours before the alarm. Turning it off, moving it later, adding a snooze: refused. You decided last night, when you could still think.

First, light.

For ten minutes the screen brightens without a sound. Scan your tag now and it won't ring at all.

Try to turn it off.

Drag the phone onto the copper tag. Nothing else will stop it, not even scrolling away.

bathroom mirror some other tag

Still up?

Seven minutes after the scan it asks. No answer within a minute, and it rings again, this time without snoozes.

The small print of getting up

  • Any sticker

    Pair any NFC tag with a fixed ID. Nothing is written to it. Use one, a random one each morning, or all of them in order.

  • Snoozes that shrink

    Five minutes, then three, then one. Or make it scan a tag even for a snooze.

  • Every day its own time

    Several alarms, one time per weekday. Skip the next one, or ring at another time just once.

  • Streaks, kept private

    How long you took and how often you snoozed. It never leaves the phone.

  • Lost the tag?

    After a minute of ringing there is a way out: type eleven sentences, about five minutes, while it keeps ringing.

  • Survives a reboot

    It rings before the first unlock, and up to 30 minutes late if the phone was off.

  • Nothing to sign up for

    No account, no server, no internet permission at all. Send your setup to a friend as a file, without your tags.

Make it yours

You need

  • A phone with Android 13 or newer and NFC
  • An NFC sticker, for example an NTAG213 or NTAG215
  • JDK 17 or newer and the Android SDK, to build it

Build and install

git clone https://github.com/AmineChr54/lockin-alarm-android-app
cd lockin-alarm-android-app
./gradlew assembleRelease
adb install -r app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk

Then open it, follow “Before it works”, pair your tag and run the silent test ring.

What it can't stop

You. Force-stopping the app, uninstalling it or switching the phone off still works. Android doesn't let an app prevent that, and it shouldn't.
